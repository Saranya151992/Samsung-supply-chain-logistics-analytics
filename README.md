# Samsung Supply Chain & Logistics Analytics Dashboard

Interactive Supply Chain & Logistics Dashboard tracking gross revenue, shipment delays, inventory valuations, supplier performance, and B2B customer distribution metrics for Samsung products using Power BI.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Data Visualization & Dashboarding:** Power BI Desktop
- **Data Processing & Analysis:** Power Query, DAX, Excel, SQL

## 🔑 Key Metrics Analyzed
- **Financial Performance:** Gross Revenue ($186.86M), Net Revenue ($176.95M), Gross Profit ($48.56M), Profit Margin (27.44%)
- **Volume & Fulfillment:** Total Sales Quantity (78.13M), Order Quantity (129K), Total Shipments (8K), Shipment Volume (3M)
- **Inventory & Suppliers:** Inventory Value (160K), Average Supplier Lead Time (9–12 days across key vendors like BOE Technology, Sony Semiconductor, TSMC, SK Hynix)
- **Logistics & Customers:** Delayed Shipments breakdown by logistics partners (Maersk Line, DHL, DB Schenker, FedEx) and B2B Revenue share (Amazon, Flipkart, Best Buy, MediaMarkt Saturn, Samsung Direct Store)
